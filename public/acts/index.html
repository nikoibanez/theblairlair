BLAIR'S
ALLEGED
ACTS

There is no useful chronology here. Material is filed when it surfaces, usually damp, sometimes folded into a pocket with tobacco crumbs, and frequently attached to an accusation that begins confidently before losing structural integrity.

Every act below is alleged. Blair has retained no counsel because Blair has retained almost nothing.

ENTRY 006 // 02:41

Platform Loitering With Intent to Loiter Harder

Blair was allegedly observed remaining perfectly still near the station until the witness looked away to answer a text, at which point he had advanced an unreasonable distance without improving his posture. No ticket was produced. A mustard packet was.

ALLEGED: menacing perch / condiment possession / hsssssss

ENTRY 005 // LATE

Doorway Occupation During Business-Like Hours

A figure matching Blair's least flattering description spent part of the evening near a doorway, neither entering nor leaving, apparently engaged in the advanced rat-man discipline of making a normal threshold feel personally accusatory.

ALLEGED: doorway misuse / prolonged dampness

ENTRY 004 // BEFORE ANYONE ADMITTED SEEING HIM

Roof Perching, Menacingly

The report begins with the phrase "I knew I saw him before I knew his name" and deteriorates from there. Blair was allegedly perched above the street with enough confidence to suggest ownership and too little balance to support it. Eye contact was reported immediately before the witness entered a bush involuntarily.

ALLEGED: perching / staring / bush-related terror

ENTRY 003 // DAWN-ISH

Unauthorized Beer Can Geometry

Three cans appeared inside a locked stone structure in an arrangement nobody had the confidence to call ritualistic. Blair's involvement remains unproven, although one can had been crushed in a manner described as "too deliberate for weather."

ALLEGED: trespass / arrangement / poor brand loyalty

ENTRY 002 // AFTER MIDNIGHT

Alley Hissing Without Clear Provocation

The audio is useless except for rain, a distant vehicle, and seventeen seconds of sustained hissing that becomes louder after the recorder says, "It's probably a pipe." The pipe has declined comment.

ALLEGED: hssssssssssss / wet-place occupation

ENTRY 001 // FILE OPENED

General Presence With No Defensible Explanation

This is the oldest surviving entry and contains almost no usable information beyond a building photograph, a coffee stain, and the handwritten instruction "CHECK ABOVE THE SECOND WINDOW." Nothing is visible above the second window. Everyone checks anyway.

ALLEGED: being there / possibly not being there
